# Learn Compos

The canonical learn-by-doing tutorial is bundled at
[`apps/compos_core/priv/tutorials/COMPOS`](../apps/compos_core/priv/tutorials/COMPOS).

Inside Compos, press `C-h t` or choose **Start the tutorial** on the Welcome
page. Compos opens a safe, editable copy and keeps the bundled master intact.
